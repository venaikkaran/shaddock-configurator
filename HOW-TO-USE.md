# How to Use the Shaddock Home Options Website

## What This Is

This is a website that shows you **all the upgrade options** for our new Shaddock home. You can check off the ones you want, see how much they cost, and make sure we stay within our budget.

**Your choices are saved automatically.** You can close the browser and come back later -- everything will still be there.

---

## Getting Started

1. **Open the website** in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox on your laptop.
2. **The first time you visit**, a quick tutorial will walk you through everything. Just click "Next" to go through each step.
3. **If you ever need help again**, click the big **red "Help" button** at the bottom-right corner of the screen.

---

## The 3 Main Parts of the Screen

```
 LEFT SIDE          MIDDLE              RIGHT SIDE
 ──────────         ──────────          ──────────
 Categories         Options List        Your Budget
 (click one to      (check the ones     (running total
  see its options)   you want)           of what you
                                         picked)
```

### Left Side: Categories
- Lists all the types of upgrades (Electrical, Tile, Cabinets, etc.)
- **Click a category** to see its options in the middle
- A small number shows how many items you picked in each category

### Middle: Options
- Shows all the options for the category you picked
- **Check the box** next to an option to add it
- **Uncheck it** to remove it
- Some items have **round buttons** instead of checkboxes -- that means you pick only ONE from that group
- **Gray items** marked "Included" are already part of the base home (you don't need to do anything with those)
- **"Price TBD"** items don't have a price yet -- the builder will fill these in later

### Right Side: Budget
- Type your budget amount at the top
- The colored bar shows how you're doing:
  - **Green** = under budget (good!)
  - **Yellow** = getting close
  - **Red** = over budget
- Below the bar, you can see how much you're spending in each category

## How to Browse Options

You have two ways to browse:

### By Room (recommended for most people)
Click **"By Room"** in the top bar. This groups options by where they go in your house:
- Master Suite
- Kitchen
- Living Areas
- Bedrooms
- Bathrooms
- Exterior
- etc.

### By Trade
Click **"By Trade"** in the top bar. This groups options by type of work:
- Electrical
- Plumbing
- Tile
- Cabinets
- etc.

---

## How to Pick Your Elevation

At the top of the screen, there's an **"Elevation"** dropdown. Your home plan comes in different front-face styles (A, B, C, D, or E). Some options and prices change depending on which elevation you have.

- If you know your elevation, pick it from the dropdown
- If you don't know yet, leave it on **"All Elevations"**

---

## Searching for Something Specific

There's a **search box** at the top of the screen. Type any word (like "granite", "faucet", or "wood floor") and it will instantly show you every matching option.

Click the **X** next to the search box to clear the search and go back to browsing.

---

## Saving Your Choices

Your choices save automatically every time you make a change. But if you want to save a **named version** (so you can make a different list and compare them):

1. Scroll down on the right side (Budget panel)
2. Click **"Save Configuration"**
3. Give it a name (like "Budget Friendly" or "Dream List")
4. Click Save

To load a saved version, click on it in the list. To delete one, click the trash icon.

---

## Comparing Two Versions

Want to see two saved lists side by side?

1. Click **"Compare"** in the top bar
2. Check the boxes next to the versions you want to compare
3. Click **"Compare Selected"**
4. You'll see the differences highlighted
5. Click **"Exit Compare"** when you're done

---

## Wizard Mode (Step-by-Step)

If all the categories feel overwhelming, try **Wizard** mode:

1. Click **"Wizard"** in the top bar
2. It walks you through **one category at a time**
3. Click **"Next"** to move to the next category
4. Click **"Skip"** to skip a category you're not interested in
5. Click **"Back"** to go back

---

## Tips

- **You can't break anything.** Feel free to check and uncheck options as much as you want.
- **Everything saves automatically.** You won't lose your work if you close the browser.
- **Use the search** if you're looking for something specific -- it's the fastest way.
- **The red Help button** is always there at the bottom-right if you get lost.
- **Gray "Included" items** are already part of your home. You don't need to select them.
- **Items marked "Price TBD"** means the builder hasn't set a price yet. You can't add these to your budget, but you can see what's available.

## Quick Reference

| What you want to do | How to do it |
|---|---|
| See kitchen options | Click "By Room" then click "Kitchen" on the left |
